Weight Room

Every member on one screen, each with today's workout and their own loads. Walk a panel through its steps as the room moves, and check the session off when it's done. Coaches only.

Custom Workouts

Workouts you build yourself: blocks of exercises, rounds, and rests. Hit Run it and the screen walks the room through every step. Big enough to read across the weight room. Coaches only.

Programs

Training templates you build once and reuse: lay out the weeks, then apply a program to a group (or all of them) from any start date. Applying writes coach overrides. The season plan itself never changes. Coaches only.

Team Calendar

The whole team on one month: every group's sessions exactly as Today and the checklist resolve them, with meet and test days marked. Coaches only.

Data

The whole team dataset, in and out: one backup file for safekeeping, restores that merge instead of wiping, and spreadsheets for everything else. Coaches only.

Devices

The testing hardware: import a ForceDecks or NordBord results file from VALD Hub, key in a T-APEX session by hand, and every number lands in the athlete's testing history, labeled by device. Coaches only.

## How to fill it in

1. After practice, open `week_01_log.csv`.
2. In that day's row, fill `actual_session_ids` (use the same codes if the plan
   ran as written), `what_actually_happened`, `times_or_marks`, and
   `how_it_felt_1_to_5`.
3. Anything that changed (cut short, swapped, rained out) goes in `notes`.
   The stop rule from the plan: if times are 3 percent or more off standard
   or form breaks down, the high session ends — write that down, it counts.
